Design Philosophy

Luminate is built to be vibrant, accessible, and performance-first. Minimal JavaScript, reusable Liquid components, and blazing-fast loads. The font hierarchy is built around Poppins Thin, Light, Regular, and Bold to maintain clarity with elegance.
