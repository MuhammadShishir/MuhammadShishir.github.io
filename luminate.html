Luminate Theme – Devlog & Documentation

v0.0.0.6

Released: June 2025 • Author: MKShishir

Introduced key layout elements: Header, Footer, Hero Section, Room Light/Color Mood Changer, Best Seller & New Arrivals. Enhanced with basic animation and clean visual structure.

Design Philosophy

Luminate is built to be vibrant, accessible, and performance-first. Minimal JavaScript, reusable Liquid components, and blazing-fast loads. The font hierarchy is built around Poppins Thin, Light, Regular, and Bold to maintain clarity with elegance.
