Design Philosophy

Luminate is built to be vibrant, accessible, and performance-first. Minimal JavaScript, reusable Liquid components, and blazing-fast loads. The typography hierarchy relies on clean weight variations (Thin, Light, Regular, Bold) to maintain pristine visual legibility.
